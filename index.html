About Odisha Hydro Power Corporation

The Odisha Hydro Power Corporation Ltd. (OHPC) is a Gold Category State Public Sector Undertaking of the Government of Odisha. Consequent to the reform in the power sector, OHPC was incorporated in 1995 to operate, maintain, and manage hydro generating stations across the state of Odisha.

With massive investments in green energy, hydel projects, and grid security, OHPC handles a wide array of high-security drawings, operational logbooks, tender schedules, and statutory regulatory filings. DocShield serves as the next-generation document vaults platform ensuring that citizen files remain accessible while security-critical operations are fully encrypted.

Primary System Principles:

  • Strict RBAC Encryption: Cryptographic pipeline visualizer enforces AES-256 and RSA key signature verification for proprietary designs.
  • Compliance Audit Logs: Cryptographically verified system logging of all internal reads and writes.
  • Citizen Access Transparency: Direct download of citizen charters and tenders without system accounts.
